🎉 **Congratulations to Dr. Alice Tang!** 🎉

We are thrilled to announce that our postdoctoral researcher **Alice Tang** has been selected for the prestigious [**Forbes 30 Under 30 in Science (2026)**](https://www.forbes.com/profile/alice-tang/) list!

## From Forbes Editor:

*"Alice Tang is using AI computational tools to understand and diagnose complex diseases like Alzheimer's. A physician, engineer and scientist, she pioneered methods to analyze millions of health records using AI, uncovering revelations about diseases, such as the fact that dementia presents differently in men and women. Her ultimate goal? Better diagnostics and personalized therapies, with applications from neurodegeneration to autoimmune disorders. Tang's work has been published in Nature Aging, Nature Medicine, and Science Translational Medicine and garnered more than 800 citations."*

**Congratulations, Alice! We're proud to have you as part of our team.** 🧠🤖
