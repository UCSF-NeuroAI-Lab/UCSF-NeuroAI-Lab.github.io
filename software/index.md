# Software

### ChatAD — Alzheimer's Disease Research Assistant

ChatAD is an AI-powered research assistant for Alzheimer's Disease. It searches ADNI documentation — including protocols, policies, consent forms, and procedures — and browses over 322 datasets from the [AD Data Initiative (ADDI)](https://www.alzheimersdata.org/){:target="_blank"} catalogue, helping researchers quickly find relevant data for their studies. Sources are cited in every response.
