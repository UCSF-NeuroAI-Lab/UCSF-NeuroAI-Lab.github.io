# UCSF NeuroAI Lab

_**Bridging Neuroscience and AI to Transform Brain Health**_

The **UCSF NeuroAI Lab**, directed by Pedro Pinheiro-Chagas, PhD, bridges fundamental cognitive neuroscience with state-of-the-art artificial intelligence to tackle two interrelated challenges: revolutionizing the diagnosis and treatment of neurodegenerative diseases and elucidating the neural substrates of uniquely human symbolic cognition.

Located at the [UCSF Memory and Aging Center](https://memory.ucsf.edu/), Department of Neurology, our lab employs an interdisciplinary approach—spanning cognitive neuroscience, computational modeling, and AI-driven clinical research—to deepen our theoretical understanding of human cognition while enhancing our capacity to diagnose and treat disorders of the mind.

## Our Research Focus

We develop **advanced AI systems** designed to optimize the **discovery, diagnosis, and care of neurodegenerative diseases**. Our work combines generative AI with traditional machine learning to analyze multimodal clinical data, emphasizing **complex reasoning**, **model interpretability**, and **scientific grounding**.

### Key Research Areas

* **[Agentic AI Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.06842)** - Reasoning about vast multimodal datasets to accelerate disease discovery
* **Copilot Decision Support** - Scaling specialist-level clinical and neuropathological diagnoses with AI
* **AI Voice Agents** - Conducting clinical assessments to streamline data collection, monitoring and care
* **Cognitive Neuroscience** - Investigating the neural basis of symbolic systems like mathematics and language

### Recent Publications

* **[Integrating Generative Artificial Intelligence in ADRD: A Roadmap for Streamlining Diagnosis and Care](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.06842)** - Perspective paper on AI in neurodegenerative disease
* **[Characterizing Dementia Phenotypes from Unstructured EHR Notes](https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.10.01.25336815)** - Leveraging generative AI and interpretable ML for clinical discovery
* **[Agentic AI System for Classification of Primary Progressive Aphasia Variants](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.10.28.25338977v3)** - Multi-agent architecture for specialist-level diagnosis

## Our Approach

We leverage cutting-edge methodologies across multiple domains:

* **Advanced AI Systems**: Developing agentic AI models that integrate multimodal clinical data
* **Innovative Behavioral Tracking**: Using continuous trajectory analysis to reveal cognitive processes
* **High-Resolution Neuroimaging**: Employing MEG, fMRI, and intracranial EEG for precise neural mapping
* **Computational Modeling**: Creating models that capture the dynamics of human cognition

## Collaboration & Awards

Our flagship project to augment clinical care with AI copilot systems was recently awarded a [**UCSF Catalyst Program**](https://innovation.ucsf.edu/2025-catalyst-awardees) grant—a translational accelerator focused on advancing UCSF discoveries for patient benefit and commercialization.

We collaborate closely with leading researchers across:
- UCSF Memory and Aging Center
- Bakar Computational Health Sciences Institute
- Center for Intelligent Imaging (ci2)
- Weill Institute for Neuroscience

## Research Impact

↻ **Insights from neuroscience** help us power our AI systems, which recursively help us better understand the brain ↺

Our work aims to:
- Accelerate early detection of neurodegenerative diseases
- Provide specialist-level diagnostic support to underserved populations
- Advance understanding of human cognitive architecture
- Develop clinically actionable AI tools that enhance individualized care

## Technologies & Methods

We combine cutting-edge AI with advanced neuroscience techniques:

* **Generative AI**: Large language models, multi-agent systems, agentic AI architectures
* **Traditional ML**: Interpretable machine learning, ensemble methods, dimensionality reduction
* **Multimodal Data**: Clinical notes, neuroimaging (MRI, MEG, fMRI), neuropsychological assessments, EHR data
* **Neurophysiology**: Intracranial EEG, electrical brain stimulation, continuous behavioral tracking
