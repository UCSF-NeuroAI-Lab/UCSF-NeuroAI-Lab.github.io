## LinkedIn Post

**Announcing the UCSF NeuroAI Lab**

Earlier this year, we launched the UCSF NeuroAI Lab at the Memory and Aging Center, Department of Neurology, Weill Institute for Neurosciences. 

Our mission: Develop AI systems to accelerate the discovery, diagnosis, and care of neurodegenerative diseases.

**Why this matters:**
- Approximately 6 million Americans live with neurodegenerative diseases, projected to reach 12 million by 2060
- Only about 20% of patients see a dementia specialist
- Over half of primary care diagnoses occur at severe disease stages
- FDA-approved treatments work best in early stages, but access to timely diagnosis remains uneven

We believe in AI as Scalable Privilege (Atul Butte).

**Our focus areas:**

**Foundation Health Models:** Using vast clinical datasets to uncover disease subtypes, risk factors, and progression patterns

**Decision Support Systems:** Delivering specialist-level diagnoses at scale (recently awarded a UCSF Catalyst Award)

**AI Conversational Agents:** Automating clinical assessments to streamline data collection and extend care to underserved areas

We are fortunate to be embedded within one of the world's most comprehensive dementia research programs, with approximately 5,000 deeply phenotyped patients, 10,000+ annotated clinical notes, and an 850-specimen brain bank.

Check out our recent publications:

**Integrating Generative Artificial Intelligence in ADRD: A Roadmap for Streamlining Diagnosis and Care in Neurodegenerative Diseases**
Our perspective on how generative AI can address systemic barriers in dementia diagnosis and care, from foundation models to conversational agents.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.06842

**Review of Artificial Intelligence for Clinical Use in Alzheimer's Disease and Related Dementias**
A comprehensive review for clinicians on how AI is being applied across the dementia care continuum, from early detection to treatment monitoring.
https://doi.org/10.1055/a-2744-9871

**Characterizing Dementia Phenotypes from Unstructured EHR Notes with Generative AI and Interpretable Machine Learning**
We combine LLMs with interpretable ML to extract clinical features from millions of notes, identifying subtypes and risk factors for AD and FTD.
https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.10.01.25336815

**Agentic Generative Artificial Intelligence System for Classification of Pathology-Confirmed Primary Progressive Aphasia Variants**
A multi-agent AI system that integrates clinical notes, neuropsych testing, and MRI to classify PPA variants down to the neuropathology with specialist-level accuracy and superhuman speed.
https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.10.28.25338977

Grateful for support from NIH, Eli Lilly, Gates Ventures, UCSF Dyslexia Center, and UCSF Multitudes.

We have several open positions for clinical research coordinators, data scientists, and postdocs. Reach out if you are interested in joining our team!
